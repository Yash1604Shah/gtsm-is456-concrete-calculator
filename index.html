3. What the calculation means

Three-cube sample validity: Under Clause 15.4, the test result of a sample is the mean of three specimens, provided no specimen deviates from that mean by more than ±15%. Invalid sample results must be investigated before any acceptance conclusion.

General four-result case: For grades M15 and above, the required mean of four non-overlapping consecutive valid sample results is at least fck + the greater of [0.825 × standard deviation, rounded to nearest 0.5 MPa] and 3 MPa. The required minimum for each valid sample result is fck − 3 MPa (IS 456 Table 11, as amended).

Small pour: For concrete quantity up to 30 m³ when fewer than four samples are required, the mean of the required sample results must be at least fck + 4 MPa; the individual minimum is fck − 2 MPa for 2–3 samples. Where exactly one sample is required, that sample itself must meet fck + 4 MPa. Do not select this option for a larger pour or an incomplete sample series.
